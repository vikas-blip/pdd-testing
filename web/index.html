Medication Safety Guide

1. 🌡️ Temperature Storage Rules

Store Insulin & Biologics in refrigerator (2°C–8°C). Never freeze medications.

2. ⏰ Consistent Dosage Timing

Take daily doses at the same time each day for maximum therapeutic efficacy.

3. 🚫 Expiry Check

Discard any expired pills or opened liquid suspensions past 14 days.

4. 💧 Hydration & Administration

Swallow tablets with a full glass of water. Avoid taking meds with alcohol or grapefruit juice.
